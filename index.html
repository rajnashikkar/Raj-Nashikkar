Prior Authorization: Understanding the Process Behind Every Healthcare Request.

Prior Authorization is a health insurance review process in which a payer evaluates whether a requested medication, diagnostic test, procedure or healthcare service meets the applicable coverage and clinical requirements before the service is provided. Authorization does not automatically guarantee payment: final payment may still depend on eligibility, benefits, coverage terms and claim adjudication.
